# Next boss: Rocco Vendetta

Inspired by Ricardo from Limbus Company, renamed to **Rocco Vendetta**. The **2nd hardest boss** (after the Kraken).
Not coded yet. Werner's moves are still to come from the owner.

## Fight
- **3 phases.**
- **At half health** he summons his little brother **Werner** (300 HP), who fights alongside him. *(Werner's moves: TBD.)*

## Moveset
1. **Kick**: inflicts a 3-second stun.
2. **Punch**: inflicts a 3-second stun.
3. **Counter / Payback**: counter-attack.
4. **Assemble**: summons minions that fight for him.
5. **Watch Your Back!**: teleports behind you, hits your back, then switches behind you again.
6. **MY HAIR COUPOOOOOOOOOOONS!!!**: a charged nuke around him, 10-15 blocks wide. Fast reactions can run out of it.
   Getting hit = lose half your HP and a 1-second stun.
7. **COMPLETE AND TOTAL EXTERMINATION!!!**: leaps into the air and lands on a random player: a one-shot, no matter what.
   Only usable when he has **max passives** (50 Vengeance Tattoos).
8. **Swear Vengeance**: dodges every attack for 5 seconds and gains 3 buffs.
9. **No More Tests**: hits 3 people.

## His passives
- Kicks and punches stun for 3 seconds.
- **Vengeance Tattoo**: every time he's hit he gains one, up to 50. (Max = unlocks Complete and Total Extermination.)

## When he dies
Players get **his passives** (as an item/ability; design TBD) plus:
- **Cosmetics** (one of): **Golden Chains**, **his Book**, or **his Coat**. Unlocked forever in FaultlineCosmetics: `cosmetic unlock <player> rocco_chains|rocco_book|rocco_coat`. The art already exists.
- **Weapon: Fist**: lets you use every move he has, each on a cooldown. With the Fist you also get:
  - **Mercy of the Big Brother**: buffs you a little, does no damage.
  - **TRICKED ME, DID YOU??!!**: buffs you, does no damage.
  - **Ah, You Were One of the Fam**: shields you, but costs health. Once per boss fight.
- **50-150 Mythic Goodie Bags**
- **10 Goodie Bags**

# 🎈 FAULTLINE SMP UPDATE: HOT AIR BALLOONS 🎈

## 🎈 Hot Air Balloons
Craft one: **5 wool** (top row and sides), a **Campfire** in the middle, **String, Barrel, String** along the bottom.
- Right-click the ground to set it up
- Step into the basket and right-click to fly
- **Jump** = rise, **Sneak** = sink (and get out once you've landed), **W A S D** = go where you look, **Sprint** = faster
- The burner needs fuel while you're in the air: **Coal / Charcoal** 1 min, **Blaze Rod** 2.5 min, **Coal Block** 10 min (right-click with it)
- Out of fuel? It slowly sinks back down
- Right-click it with a **dye** to change its colour
- **Sneak + punch** inside the basket to pack it back up

## ☠️ Hardcore Boss Rush
Sneak + right-click your Boss Rush Sigil (or `/bossrush start hardcore`). Every boss, back to back, and **one death means the whole team is out**. Logging out counts too. Win it for **double rewards**, its own leaderboard (`/bossrush top hardcore`) and the "No Second Chances" achievement.

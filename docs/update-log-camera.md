# 🎥 FAULTLINE SMP UPDATE: CAMERA, DISCORD & BEDROCK 🎥

## 🎥 Camera Mode
`/camera` turns you into a free camera: fly anywhere (through walls too), and nobody can see you. Type `/camera` again and you're back exactly where you were. Great for recording, on Bedrock too.
- `/camera speed 1-10` to change how fast you fly
- You can go up to 64 blocks from where you started
- Not right after a fight (10 seconds), and not during races

## 📊 /stats in Discord
Type `/stats <player>` in our Discord and the bot answers with their stats: kingdom, online or last seen, play time, kills, deaths, K/D, kill streak, tier, mob kills, Index progress, bosses defeated and achievements.

## 💡 Suggestions
Got an idea for the server? `/suggest <your idea>` in game posts it in the Discord, where everyone can react to vote on it.

## ⚔️ Friendly Fire Vote
Each kingdom now decides if its members can hurt each other.
- `/kingdom ff on` or `/kingdom ff off` to vote
- `/kingdom ff` to see where the vote stands
Once 3 or more members have voted, the majority wins.

## 💀 Boss Death Messages
Dying to a boss now says what got you: "was frozen solid by Frostbeard", "was dragged into the deep by the Leviathan", "owed Rocco Vendetta money"... instead of "was slain by Slime".

## 📱 Bedrock
- `/cosmetics` is a proper Bedrock menu now: pick a slot, tap to wear, buy from the Seasonal Shop
- `/kingdom` is a Bedrock menu too: link your Discord by typing your name and code in a box, vote on friendly fire, see who's in each kingdom

## 🐛 Bug Fixes
- `/stats` in game shows your kingdom again, and friendly-fire kills are logged again

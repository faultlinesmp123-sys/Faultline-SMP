# ⚔️ FAULTLINE SMP UPDATE: ELITES, RANGERS & TRINKETS ⚔️

## ★ Elite Mobs
1 in 100 monsters now spawns as a gold **★ Elite**: double health, hits harder, faster and a bit bigger.
Kill one for **double drops, triple XP and bonus loot** (diamonds, emeralds, golden apples, sometimes a Goodie Bag).

## 🧟 Mutant Mobs
- **Looter Zombie**: its hits can steal a stack from your backpack (never your hotbar or armor). Kill it and you get it all back.
- **Blazing Skeleton**: fire-proof, and its arrows set you on fire.
- **Splitting Creeper**: explodes (or dies) and splits into Mini Creepers.

**Raids** get Elites and Mutants too. If a raid ends while a Looter still has your stuff, it drops it.

## 🌾 Farming Seasons
Every crop has real-life months it's in season: it grows faster and harvests give more. Out of season it grows slower.
`/crops` shows what's in season right now (pumpkins in October!).

## 🐴 Horse Stats
Right-click a horse, donkey, mule, llama or camel with a **stick** to see its speed, jump height and health, with star ratings.

## ☠️ Death Coordinates
When you die, only you get told exactly where, with a button to copy the coordinates. `/lastdeath` shows it again any time.

## 💬 Discord
- Messages in the Discord chat channel now show up in game.
- Every morning the Discord gets yesterday's stats: top killer, most deaths, longest playtime, mob slayer, rarest find, bosses beaten and more.

## 📕 Boss Books on Bows
**Frostbite, Venom, Lifesteal and Thunderstrike** books now go on **bows** too (item left, book right in an anvil).
They work through your arrows on a strong draw.

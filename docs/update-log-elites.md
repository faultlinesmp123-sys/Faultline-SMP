# ⚔️ FAULTLINE SMP UPDATE: ELITES, MUTANTS & FARMING SEASONS ⚔️

## ★ Elite Mobs
Watch out for gold **★ Elite** names. 1 in 100 monsters spawns as an Elite: double health, harder hits, faster.
Kill one for **double drops, triple XP and bonus loot** (diamonds, emeralds, golden apples, sometimes a Goodie Bag).

## 🧟 Mutants
- **Looter Zombie**: its hits can steal a stack from your backpack. Kill it and you get everything back.
- **Blazing Skeleton**: fire-proof, and its arrows set you on fire.
- **Splitting Creeper**: blows up (or dies) and splits into Mini Creepers.

Elites and mutants show up in **raids** now too. They're all in the Faultline Index.

## 🌾 Farming Seasons
Every crop has real-life months it's in season. In season it grows faster and harvests give more; out of season it grows slower.
Pumpkins in October, wheat in summer... `/crops` shows what's in season right now.

## 🐴 Horse Stats
Right-click any horse, donkey, mule, llama or camel with a **stick** to see its speed, jump height and health, with star ratings.

## ☠️ Death Coordinates
When you die, you (and only you) get told exactly where, with a button to copy the coordinates. `/lastdeath` shows it again any time.

## 💬 Discord
- Messages in our Discord chat channel now show up in game.
- Every morning the Discord gets yesterday's stats: top killer, most deaths, longest playtime, rarest find, bosses beaten and more.

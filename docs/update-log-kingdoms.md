# 👑 FAULTLINE SMP UPDATE: KINGDOMS 👑

## 👑 Kingdoms
Every player now belongs to **Kingdom 1, 2 or 3**, based on your **role in our Discord**.
**Link your account (one time):**
1. In game: `/kingdom link <your discord username>`
2. Our bot DMs you a code (turn on DMs from server members if it can't)
3. In game: `/kingdom verify <code>`
Your kingdom shows next to your name in tab and updates by itself if your role changes.
- `/kingdom`: your kingdom and any grace periods
- `/kingdom list 1`: who's in a kingdom

## 🛡️ Grace Periods
Staff can turn on grace for **everyone** or for **one kingdom**. While your kingdom has grace, nobody can hurt you, and you can't attack other players either. When a grace period starts or ends, it's announced in chat. If yours is on, you'll see a timer in your action bar.

## ⚡ Performance
Fixed several things that were making the server lag, especially around big storage rooms full of shulkers.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes
- Boss Rush no longer drops enchant books or music discs for every stage
- Blood Moon raids can't spawn two Bulwarks
- Painting a ship and then scrapping it gives back what it was built from (no more wood or wool swapping)
- Open races no longer pull ship seats off their ships
- Lent race rockets and elytras can't be hidden in bundles
- Land cannons drop as an item if the block under them disappears

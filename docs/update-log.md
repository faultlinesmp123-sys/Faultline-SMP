# ⚡ FAULTLINE SMP UPDATE ⚡

## 🛡️ Combat & Balance
- **Spawn protection:** after you respawn, other players can't hurt you for **30 seconds**. Hitting a player ends it early, so no fighting from safety.
- **Vampires nerfed:** claws 9 → 5 damage, health 40 → 30, weaker bleeding, and far fewer of them during Blood Moons.
- **Blood Moon raids:** raids during a Blood Moon have **25% more mobs**, and those mobs are tougher and stronger. Win one and every defender gets an **extra Mythic Bag**.

## 📖 Boss Enchant Books
Bosses now drop enchantments vanilla doesn't have. Put your item on the left of an anvil and the book on the right. Two books of the same level make the next level.
- **Lifesteal I–II** (swords/axes/tridents): heal from the damage you deal
- **Frostbite I–II:** a chance to slow what you hit
- **Venom I–II:** a chance to poison
- **Thunderstrike I:** a chance to call lightning for bonus damage
- **Vitality I–II** (chestplates): +2 / +4 max health
- **Featherstep I** (boots): less fall damage, never trample crops
- **Vein Miner I** (pickaxes): sneak while mining an ore to dig out the whole vein
Each boss has its own books. Check the **Faultline Index** to see who drops what.

## 🏁 Races
Staff can now host races. Click **[JOIN]** in chat when one opens.
- **Elytra races:** fly through the rings (elytra and rockets can be lent)
- **Horse races:** everyone gets the same horse, so the best rider wins
- **Boat races:** boats provided
- **Open races:** bring anything, even your own ship
Best times are saved: `/race top`. The top 3 win prizes.

## 🎣 Fishing
- Every fish you catch now has a **weight**.
- Beat the server record for that kind of fish and everyone hears about it. Your fish becomes a **Trophy Fish** with your name on it.
- **Fishing Tournaments:** your single best catch counts, scored against the biggest size for that kind (so a huge Tropical Fish can beat a medium Salmon). The top 3 win prizes.
- `/fishing records` · `/fishing top`

## ⛵ Ships
- **Ship upgrades** (craftable; right-click your ship to fit one):
  - **Hull Plating:** +15% health, stacks 3×
  - **Swift Sails:** +10% speed, stacks 3×
  - **Quick Loaders:** cannons reload faster, stacks 2×
  - **Iron Ram:** your rams hurt you half as much and them 50% more
- **Land cannons:** place a Ship Cannon on any block (castle walls, docks, cliffs). Stand next to it holding a cannonball and it **aims where you look**, with an arc showing where it lands. Right-click to fire.
- **Paint jobs:** right-click your ship with a **dye** to recolour the sails, or **sneak + right-click with planks** to change the whole hull to that wood.
- **Pirate treasure maps:** sometimes found when you plunder a skeleton ship. Follow the map, find the **red X**, dig, and claim the buried chest.

## 🪑 Quality of Life

- **/report <player> <reason>:** sends a report straight to the staff, with locations.

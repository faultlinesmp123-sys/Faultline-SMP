**HALCYON - the city under the sea** (Deep 1.17.0, Bosses 2.1.0, Index 1.9.9, Items 1.3.14)

There's a city in the Dark Ocean now. Way out in the south-west (around -1800, 2200), under a huge glass dome, people actually live there.

**The city**
- A massive glass dome with a plaza, a fountain with a diver statue, bubble tubes, houses all around a ring road, farms, a tree grove, a coal mine under the floor, a building site, a market, a tavern (the Kelp & Kettle), guard barracks and a dock.
- It has its own music. Walk in and it switches to the Bio-Center Labs theme.
- Swim in through the south gate (the dock) or the west gate.

**The people**
- Every citizen is a villager with a name, a house, a bed and a job. They wake up, eat breakfast, go to work, half of them grab lunch at the tavern, hang out on the plaza in the evening and go home to sleep in their own beds.
- Farmers actually harvest and replant the crops, woodcutters chop trees and stack logs, miners go down the mine and dig coal, builders build a whole house block by block (then it fails inspection and they start over lol), scientists work the lab, aquarists feed the tanks, merchants run the market stalls, kids run around the plaza.
- Right click them to trade, normal villager style trades depending on their job.
- You can't leash them, ride them, name them or steal them.

**Don't be stupid**
- Hit a citizen and they scream, everyone nearby runs home, and you're WANTED.
- Guards run you down, hit hard and shoot you from range.
- Trying to mine the dome? If a guard (or anyone close) sees you, you're wanted. You can't break anything in the city anyway.
- Wanted goes 1 to 3 stars. Dying clears it, otherwise it wears off after a few minutes.

**Patrol subs**
- 4 subs piloted by villagers go out exploring, scanning creatures, capturing the harmless ones for the aquarium and torpedoing the dangerous ones.
- They have radar. If you're wanted anywhere within 400 blocks, they all come for you with homing torpedoes.
- Each one has a health bar. Get it to 0 and it sinks, the pilot bails out and you get salvage. You'll also be 3 stars wanted, good luck.

**Submersible**
- You can't craft the Submersible anymore. Buy it from the Dock Master at the dock office for 128 emeralds.

**The Aquarium**
- Every single Dark Ocean creature swimming around in giant tanks, animated like in the wild.
- The Bestiary on the lecterns tells you what every creature does AND where it came from. Sneak + right click to take a copy.

**The Bio-Center Lab**
- Tiny versions of every boss in specimen jars, with the real boss models, plus a giant green tank with the Abyssal Titan in it.
- Each jar has a plaque, and the lab's lectern has the Research Notes with the lore behind every boss.

**Also**
- Every biome in the Dark Ocean has its own particles and sounds now (embers, spores, marine snow in the Abyss, acid haze, ash in the Trench, snow on the Frozen Shelf, rainbow glints on the Glass Reef...). Works in places you've already explored too.
- Sea bosses flash red when you hit them.
- 2 new achievements: Welcome to Halcyon, Most Wanted.

**HALCYON 2.0** (Deep 1.18.0)

Halcyon got way bigger, and the people actually work now.

**Three domes**
- The main dome now has glass hallways to two new domes: the **Agri Dome** (south-west) and the **Guard Dome** (south-east).
- Agri Dome: big crop fields (wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot), an orchard, a barn where the farmers and woodcutters sleep, a silo, a windmill and beehives.
- Guard Dome: the barracks, an armory, a training yard with dummies, an archery range and a watchtower.
- Main dome got a Town Hall, a school with a playground, a clinic, a greenhouse, a park with a pond, a meeting bell on the plaza, and way more grass, flowers, trees and benches everywhere.

**The Aquarium**
- No more floating text.
- Every creature has its own tank now, with a sign on the glass telling you what it is and where it lives.
- New **basement** (stairs in the west aisle). The deep stuff (Abyss, caves, Sulphur Sea, Trench, the outer regions) lives down there, the Sunlit Sea creatures upstairs.

**The Mine**
- Full rebuild: a pithead shed, a big headframe tower with a winding wheel over a real shaft, a timbered stair tunnel, a bigger gallery with ore faces, rails and a smithy corner.
- New **dive room** with suit lockers and a **moon pool**. It drops into a flooded tunnel that goes all the way out to the real caves under the sea.

**The people (they actually work now)**
- In the evening they act like normal villagers on the plaza: they wander around, gossip and hang out at the bell. The kids run around and play.
- Woodcutters chop trees down for real. The whole tree comes down, they plant a sapling and it grows back a few minutes later.
- Miners crack and dig out the ore faces. The ore grows back later.
- **Divers** put on their dive helmets, jump down the moon pool, swim the flooded tunnel out to the caves, mine ore down there and swim back.
- Farmers harvest and replant the crops. Teachers teach the kids at school in the morning. Plus a doctor, a mayor, gardeners, and everyone from before.
- 53 citizens now (was 39).

**The guards (fixed)**
- If you're wanted, every guard on duty in the city comes for you, from any dome. They run the streets and hallways to reach you.
- **1 star = you get arrested**: they grab you and throw you out onto the dock, and the charge gets dropped.
- **2+ stars = they fight to kill**, swords and arrows.
- Hit a guard and he turns on you right away.
- Guards ignore anyone in creative (you get a message saying so). If you're testing, do it in survival.

**Note:** The new city only shows up where the area hasn't been generated yet. If you already visited Halcyon, staff have to reset that part of the Dark Ocean for the new version to appear.
